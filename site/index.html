Speak. It types.

Hold Ctrl Shift Space, talk, and let go. Kem Cho transcribes on your computer and pastes the text wherever your cursor is.

Download for free Try it in your browser

Private by design

Whisper runs on your machine. No account, no cloud, no audio uploaded.

Works in every app

Email, chat, docs, code editors. If it has a cursor, Kem Cho can type into it.

Cleans up as you go

Drops “um” and “uh”, fixes the words you choose, and understands “new line”.

Download

Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are published on GitHub Releases. The first launch downloads a 148 MB English speech model; after that Kem Cho works offline.

macOS (Apple silicon & Intel) Windows Linux (.deb, AppImage)

Coming next

Dictation in Hindi (हिन्दी), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી) and code-mixed Hinglish, alongside English, plus a local voice agent that talks back.

Kem Cho means “how are you?” in Gujarati. Open source under the MIT licence, inspired by Hex.
