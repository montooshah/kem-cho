Ask a question on GitHub

Is my voice sent anywhere?

No. Recording, speech recognition, the language model and the voice all run on your computer. There is no account.

Does the text appear while I speak?

Not yet. Kem Cho transcribes after you let go of the key, then pastes the whole sentence.

How long does dictation take?

On an 8-core processor with no graphics card, a 9-second English sentence took about 1 second. Hindi and Gujarati took 6 to 17 seconds.

What does the voice agent need?

Ollama, a free app that runs language models, and about 2 GB of disk for the default model.

How were the 12-sentence tests run?

In November 2026, on recordings from Google’s FLEURS test set for English, Hindi and Gujarati, and public code-switching recordings for the mixed modes.

Free dictation app for macOS, Windows and Linux

The first launch downloads the 148 MB English model once. After that, dictation works offline.

The Mac and Windows builds are not signed yet, so each system asks you to confirm the first launch.
